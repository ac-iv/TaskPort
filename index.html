Features

Built for people running many brands at once

Accounts & portfolios

Group accounts under parent companies, color-code every brand, and keep stack notes, approval chains, and brand voice where you'll actually find them.

Tasks that fit real work

Urgency, status, function, coordinator, and due date on every row. Filter down to one brand or one coordinator in a click.

Recurring sweeps, done right

Daily and weekly routines stay a single line. Check it off today, it comes back tomorrow — no endless duplicate tasks piling up.

Schedule with overdue radar

A month view colored by brand, with anything late pinned to the top so it stops being a surprise. Hover any item for the full brief.

A dashboard you'll read

Open work by account, by discipline, and by coordinator — so you know where the load actually sits before the next status call.

Links & assets attached

Pin the Drive folder, the Figma file, the brief — right on the task, labeled automatically so the list stays readable.

The task list

Everything about a task, on one line

No opening five cards to find out who owns something. Brand, coordinator, urgency, discipline, status and due date sit right there — and every column filters.

  • Status changes from a dropdown, not a guessing game
  • Each coordinator gets their own shade so scanning is instant
  • Recurring work shows its cadence instead of a fake due date
